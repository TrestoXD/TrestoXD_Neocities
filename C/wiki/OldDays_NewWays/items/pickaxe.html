Pickaxe

A pickaxe is a tiered tool that is required to mine most types of stone, ores, and blocks crafted from stone or ores. The mining level of a given pickaxe determines which blocks it can collect.


Variants

Stone Pickaxe

  • Durability - 131
  • Efficiency - 4
  • Enchantability - 5

Now stone Pickaxe is not able to mine iron! instead use the stone pickaxe to mine Copper


Copper Pickaxe

  • Durability - 169
  • Efficiency - 12
  • Enchantability - 13

The copper pickaxe is able to mine iron, and is the fastest pickaxe but! with a downfall the durability


Golden Pickaxe

  • Durability - 31
  • Efficiency - 8
  • Enchantability - 22

The Golden pickaxe is the dream of every builder! It has a native silktouch without the need of enchant it can mine your most precious blocks like stone, glass or ice without losing the item.
