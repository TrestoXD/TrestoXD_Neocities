About the Datapack

Old days new ways is developed by Tresto_XD (Vito Minaya González). It's been under developement since about Agoust 13th of 2026.

Origins

I started this datapack for a reason, I wanted to make Minecraft feel more like golden aged and more balanced, like if it was a alternative reality.

Also why a datapack and not a mod? a simple reason, if my datapack is abandonated I don't want players to get angry cuz their worlds corrupted when updating to a new version when the mod is not present.

The future

I have so many ideas for the datapack like hardening the early game with features like: harder beds, new door recipes, new structures (scary mazes with mobs, old alpha pyramids), new mobs revalanced (maybe adding even new mobs), more uses to the copper, harder glass block, Animals need grass to eat or they die and more things...
